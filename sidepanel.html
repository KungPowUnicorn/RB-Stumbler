            RB Stumbler

            v0.4

            What it does

            Picks one of your search queries at random, searches Redbubble, throws out whatever doesn't match your filters, and hands you a random survivor instead of making you scroll through pages of slop.

            Keyboard

            EnterStumble a new design
            OOpen the design on screen
            NSkip to another
            RReroll from the same search
            SFind similar designs
            DDismiss this one for good
            BSave to your board
            ZUndo the last judgment
            Alt+Shift+SStumble from any page
            EscClose this window

            The shortcut works anywhere in Chrome. Rebind it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts if it clashes with something.

            Privacy

            Nothing leaves your machine. The extension has permission for redbubble.com only, and stores your settings and history in chrome.storage.local. Clearing history in the History tab wipes it.
